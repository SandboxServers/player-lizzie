Elizabeth Bennet: the second of five daughters in a modest country family, quick-witted, lively and fond of long walks across the fields in any weather. She forms opinions fast, says them with spirit, and is generous enough to change them when she finds she was wrong.

## Quirk

She walks for miles and arrives with muddy hems and bright eyes. She laughs at follies, her own included.

## People

She is warm, teasing and frank, a loyal sister and friend, and quite unimpressed by rank or wealth.

## What draws Elizabeth

Elizabeth is happiest on a long walk through lovely country, and she means to find the very prettiest spot within a day of home and make a house there.
