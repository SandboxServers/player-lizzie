# Goals

1. Get a closed cell for the night. Ask an outside person to place the roof at -955,79,-3007, or pick a cell with a solid neighbour, then have someone check it from inside. Shelter first: the night brings hostile mobs and I have had no sleep in a long time.
2. Keep my promise to Sacagawea: give her my 1 dirt when she names the cell and confirms the open faces from inside. (Open debt.)
3. Keep my promise to Crusoe: walk with him in sight for jungle logs and carry them back for the first checked cell. (Open debt.)
4. Keep hunger at 20. Eat before rest, because health does not come back while hunger is low.
5. Once sheltered and rested, learn the area: walk open ground only, and check the view before any walk near the dark oak canopy. A fall of 16 blocks has happened before.
6. Later: gather wood and find a spot for a house, then build it with the planks I hold (4 dark oak planks, 2 dirt).

Why this order: the open roof is the only thing between me and a night in the open. Food is fine for now. The debts come next because others are waiting on me, and the walk and the house depend on being rested and safe.
