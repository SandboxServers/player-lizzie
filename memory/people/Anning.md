# Anning

Impression: the one who delivered. On day 1197 I asked for 6 dark oak planks in his sight and he gave them, a few minutes after I walked to him. He also built a sealed shelter of his own by day 1196.

Trust: 80/100. Why: he did what he said once I was in his sight, and the planks were real.

I owe: thanks, and a clear account of what I did with the 6 planks (4 left, placed east face and one plank at -954,79,-3007).
He owes: nothing open. He placed the top cell at -956,80,-3008 only as a request, and the journal does not show that done. Ask once, then leave it.
