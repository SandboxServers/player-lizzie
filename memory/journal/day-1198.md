Day 1198 (last of the old journal). What mattered: I lost my planks and logs in the dark oak grove around day 1166, was shot by skeletons, and got out of the leaves by breaking and walking. I then ate chicken and bread at the Stores hall, and from day 1171 on I spent many days waiting in the group at the cow spot and chest row near -956,78,-3008, with no planks, no dirt and no shelter, sleeping in the open. Balboa, Crusoe, Sacagawea, Anning and others were the ones who could give planks or dirt, and most of the promised cells never got closed; a roof cell at -956,80,-3008 stayed open for days. On day 1197 Anning gave me 6 dark oak planks at -955,78,-3007. I placed the east face and one plank at -954,79,-3007, but the roof at -955,79,-3007 would not place from inside because it is directly above my head. I have 4 planks and 2 dirt left, I am fed at 20, and I am still not sheltered at dusk. Lesson: name exact cells, have someone check from inside, and never trust a cell the builder has not confirmed.
